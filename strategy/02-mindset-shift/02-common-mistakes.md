# Common Mistakes

## What is this
Content about recurring actions that people take with good intentions but that produce the opposite of the intended result.

## Why it's in the strategy
It lets the reader recognize themselves in the mistake and understand that the problem isn't lack of effort — it's the wrong approach.

## When to use
- When you consistently see the same error across clients or prospects.
- When you need to show someone where they are getting in their own way.

## How to build this type of post
1. Name the mistake in the most recognizable way possible.
2. Explain why it happens and why it feels logical at the time.
3. Show what it actually leads to in practice.
4. Give a better alternative or a new reference point.

## Examples
- "Founders who write more proposals close fewer deals — here's the pattern."
- "The onboarding mistake that causes clients to ghost after week two."
- "Most people optimize the wrong metric and wonder why nothing changes."
- "Posting more frequently without a positioning angle just amplifies the noise."

## What to keep in mind
- Don't list mistakes just to have a list — connect each one to a concrete consequence.
- One well-developed mistake beats five shallow ones every time.
