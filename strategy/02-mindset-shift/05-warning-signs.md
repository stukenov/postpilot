# Warning Signs

## What is this
Content that helps someone recognize a problem, stage, or situation — specifically the moment when something needs to change.

## Why it's in the strategy
Gives the reader a way to self-identify: they see themselves in the description and their readiness to take the problem seriously increases.

## When to use
- When your audience underestimates the problem or confuses one situation with another.
- When you need to show that something has crossed from "minor" into "needs action."

## How to build this type of post
1. Pick one state, stage, or pattern to focus on.
2. List 3–5 observable signs that don't require specialist language to recognize.
3. Explain why people typically ignore or misread these signs.
4. If needed, tell the reader what to do once they recognize themselves.

## Examples
- "3 signs your positioning has drifted — and clients can feel it."
- "How to tell your pricing model is quietly costing you the best clients."
- "5 signals that a client relationship is at risk before they say anything."
- "The early signs that you're building the wrong audience for your offer."

## What to keep in mind
- Signs should be visible and behavioral, not just clinical or theoretical.
- The best formulations let the reader run a quick self-diagnosis in real time.
