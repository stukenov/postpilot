# Myth Busting

## What is this
A post where you take a widely held belief in your field and show why it's wrong — or why it dangerously oversimplifies reality.

## Why it's in the strategy
This format breaks the reader's existing lens and clears space for your approach to fill it.

## When to use
- When a persistent myth is causing your audience to make the wrong moves.
- When a mainstream piece of advice consistently leads people in the wrong direction.

## How to build this type of post
1. State the myth the way your audience actually hears it — not a strawman.
2. Explain what's wrong or missing from it.
3. Show the real cost of believing it.
4. Offer a sharper view or a working alternative.

## Examples
- "You need a big following before you can sell anything."
- "More content = more reach."
- "You need to specialize narrowly to be taken seriously as a consultant."
- "Credentials are what make clients trust you."

## What to keep in mind
- The more familiar the myth, the more people will recognize their own thinking in it.
- Never leave the bust without an alternative — the reader needs a new direction, not just a void.
