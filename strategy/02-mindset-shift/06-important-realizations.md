# Important Realizations

## What is this
A short post built around a single reframing insight that changes how the reader looks at a problem, their efforts, or their results.

## Why it's in the strategy
A compact mindset-shift format: one strong idea can trigger an internal shift without a long breakdown.

## When to use
- When you have a sharp conclusion that lands without needing a long setup.
- When you want to quickly shake the reader loose and hand them a new frame.

## How to build this type of post
1. State the core realization without preamble.
2. If needed, add one or two lines of context or example.
3. Don't mix multiple ideas into one post.
4. End where the idea has already landed — don't over-explain it into a cliche.

## Examples
- "Revenue growth that doesn't come with margin growth is just more complexity."
- "You're not avoiding the proposal conversation because you're humble. You're avoiding it because you're not sure the offer is worth it."
- "Followers don't pay invoices."
- "Momentum is a byproduct of work, not a prerequisite for starting it."
- "Silence after a pitch is also feedback."

## What to keep in mind
- The format's power comes from brevity: one insight, one shift.
- These posts work especially well between more tactical, how-to pieces.
