# Step 2. Mindset Shift

Useful content builds an audience, but it doesn't sell by itself. This step is where the reader starts to see that their current worldview, default habits, and explanations for their problem are part of the problem.

## Section focus
- Goal: Raise awareness and shake loose old beliefs.
- Role in the funnel: Move the reader from passive consumption to recognizing that the problem needs to be addressed differently.

## How to use this folder
- Publish these formats after you've already delivered value and earned attention.
- Don't push a direct sale — the job here is to let the reader reach a new conclusion on their own.
- Posts that gently reframe thinking outperform posts that argue or lecture.
