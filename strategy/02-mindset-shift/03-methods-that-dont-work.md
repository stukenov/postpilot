# Methods That Don't Work

## What is this
A post that names popular advice, tactics, or solutions in your field and explains directly why they don't produce the promised result.

## Why it's in the strategy
Clears the field of false solutions and prepares the reader to consider a different path — rather than just trying the same thing one more time.

## When to use
- When the market is saturated with polished but ineffective recommendations.
- When your audience is tired of following standard advice that never seems to work.

## How to build this type of post
1. Name the common tactic or piece of advice.
2. Explain why it sounds appealing or logical.
3. Show exactly where it breaks down in practice.
4. Point toward the principle that should replace it.

## Examples
- "Why 'just be consistent' is the worst advice for someone whose positioning is unclear."
- "Cold outreach volume doesn't fix a weak offer — it just accelerates the rejection."
- "A better website won't fix a referral problem."
- "Productivity tools don't help if you're optimizing the wrong tasks."

## What to keep in mind
- This post lands harder when you're challenging a popular recommendation, not a fringe one.
- Don't just prohibit — explain what working principle belongs in its place.
