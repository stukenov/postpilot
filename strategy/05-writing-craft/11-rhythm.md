# Rhythm

## What is this
Alternating sentence length and pace so the text doesn't drag as a monotonous block or chop as a staccato list.

## Why it's in the strategy
Rhythm sustains attention and makes even plain text feel alive, readable, and emotionally precise.

## When to use
- When the text sounds flat and pulls along without any music to it.
- When editing has made it too even and mechanical.

## How to build this type of post
1. Read the text aloud or silently in one breath.
2. If only long sentences follow each other, break one up. If only short ones, merge or extend.
3. Use short sentences for emphasis, long ones for developing a thought.
4. Don't manufacture rhythm artificially — it should reinforce the meaning.

## Examples
- After a long causal explanation, land a short punch: "That's what kills growth."
- In a case breakdown, alternate long cause-and-effect sentences with short one-line conclusions.

## What to keep in mind
- Rhythm is not a stylistic extra. It's what keeps a reader moving through the text instead of stopping.
