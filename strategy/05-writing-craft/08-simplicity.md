# Simplicity

## What is this
Phrasing ideas without ornate constructions or overloaded clauses — so the thought lands directly and clearly.

## Why it's in the strategy
The simpler an idea is expressed, the faster it registers and the less resistance the reader feels.

## When to use
- When the text starts sounding like a presentation rather than a conversation.
- When writing about a complex topic and there's a risk of sliding into jargon.

## How to build this type of post
1. Rewrite complex constructions in conversational language.
2. Replace abstract descriptions with short, clear phrases.
3. Read the text aloud — if you stumble, simplify.
4. Preserve meaning, not elegance for its own sake.

## Examples
- Instead of "The deployment of overly elaborate syntactic structures and excessive use of subordinate clauses results in diminished reader engagement" — "Complexity kills attention. Write the way you talk."

## What to keep in mind
- Simplicity makes an idea stronger, not shallower.
