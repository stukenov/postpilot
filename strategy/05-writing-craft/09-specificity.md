# Specificity

## What is this
Replacing vague words with numbers, timeframes, actions, and measurable formulations that are easy to picture.

## Why it's in the strategy
Specificity makes a claim credible and visualizable — which means it grips attention more effectively.

## When to use
- When the text contains too many loose words like "better," "faster," or "more effective."
- When a promised result needs to feel real and achievable.

## How to build this type of post
1. Find abstract spots and replace them with a number, a time, an action, or a constraint.
2. Phrase the benefit so it can be imagined and verified.
3. Don't pile on numbers for their own sake — meaningful precision is what counts.
4. Check: can you see what's described as a scene or a clear image?

## Examples
- Instead of "Drink more water" — "Drink a full glass of water within 10 minutes of waking up."
- Instead of "Write faster and more easily" — "Write a post in 20 minutes, even without inspiration."
- Instead of "Improve your focus" — "Do your most important task before checking any messages."

## What to keep in mind
- The brain responds to the measurable and the imaginable. Vague promises produce vague reactions.
