# Headline: Benefit

## What is this
The first line immediately shows what the reader will gain after reading and how it will change their situation or result.

## Why it's in the strategy
This is the most direct entry into useful content: the reader instantly understands why the time is worth spending.

## When to use
- When you have practical material with a clear, tangible outcome.
- When you need to show value quickly without building mystery first.

## How to build this type of post
1. State a clear result or change — not a topic.
2. Where possible, add a timeframe, condition, or constraint.
3. Write from the reader's perspective, not a description of your subject matter.
4. Verify that the promise is actually delivered inside the post.

## Examples
- How to say no to clients without losing the relationship.
- How to write a compelling cold message when you've never done it before.
- How to turn a side skill into a revenue stream in 60 days.

## What to keep in mind
- Benefit headlines work best when they're specific, not generic like "become better at X."
