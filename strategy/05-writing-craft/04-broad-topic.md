# Broad Topic

## What is this
Choosing a wide, plain-language framing instead of a narrow professional term that only insiders would understand.

## Why it's in the strategy
It captures attention from a wider audience and speaks the language of a potential client, not a peer reviewer.

## When to use
- When the professional framing is too narrow or technical for a general reader.
- When you need broad entry into a topic without losing the substance.

## How to build this type of post
1. Translate the expert term into everyday language.
2. Frame the topic around the reader's actual task or frustration.
3. Check that someone without a professional background understands the headline immediately.
4. Save the depth for inside the post — not in the topic label itself.

## Examples
- Instead of "Optimizing cash flow through accounts receivable management" — "How to stop running out of money even when business is good."
- Instead of "Applying agile sprint methodology to solo knowledge work" — "How to actually finish what you start every week."
- Instead of "Managing cognitive load in high-stakes client communication" — "Why your best advice keeps getting ignored."

## What to keep in mind
- Didn't understand = scrolled past. This is one of the central premises of readable content.
