# Headline: Problem

## What is this
The first line opens with conflict, pain, or a "something is wrong" situation that the brain wants resolved.

## Why it's in the strategy
A problem-led opening hooks anyone already living with that pain or afraid of it — and compels them to read through to the resolution.

## When to use
- When the post is built around a recognizable problem or crisis moment.
- When creating a sense of recognition matters more than immediately promising a benefit.

## How to build this type of post
1. Describe a situation where tension or failure is audible.
2. Frame the problem through lived experience, not a category label.
3. Signal that the post will provide a resolution, explanation, or solution.
4. Don't exaggerate the drama beyond what the content can support.

## Examples
- You hired your first employee and somehow ended up with more work, not less.
- A client paid $3,000, got results, and still left a bad review.
- You had a strong quarter, then looked at your margins and felt sick.

## What to keep in mind
- The problem must feel familiar and grounded, not abstractly catastrophic.
