# Series

## What is this
A set of posts on the same topic, published across multiple days or weeks.

## Why it's in the strategy
Lets you break a large topic into standalone pieces while building sustained interest in a single content line.

## When to use
- When the topic is too large for one post but too connected to scatter.
- When you want to progressively reveal a subject and bring the audience back.

## How to build this type of post
1. Divide the topic into logical parts, each with its own distinct thesis.
2. Make each post independently readable — no post should require the others to make sense.
3. Connect the parts through a shared question or through a promised arc.
4. Don't stretch the series past the point where the idea holds interest.

## Possible applications
- Three posts on hiring: the assumptions that break it, the common mistakes, and what a working process looks like.
- A series on starting out in a new domain: where to start, what to focus on, how to convert attention into work.

## What to keep in mind
- Each post must stand alone. A reader discovering part three should still get full value.
- End the series when the topic is done, not when an arbitrary count is reached.
