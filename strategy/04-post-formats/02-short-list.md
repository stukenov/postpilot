# Short List

## What is this
A single post up to ~500 characters with a bulleted or numbered list.

## Why it's in the strategy
Lets you deliver 3–5 points quickly — easy to scan, easy to save, no long read required.

## When to use
- When the topic breaks cleanly into short items with no explanation needed.
- When the goal is "scan fast, save fast."

## How to build this type of post
1. Write a short headline that promises a clear outcome or benefit.
2. Give 3–5 items as a plain list.
3. Keep each item brief and self-contained.
4. Close with one optional line on why this is worth saving.

## Possible applications
- Five habits that improve written communication at work.
- Three signs a current system isn't scaling anymore.

## What to keep in mind
- If any item needs a sentence of explanation, switch to the expanded list format.
- Generic lists get ignored. Each item should earn its place.
