# Carousel

## What is this
A post built around a sequence of images or short video clips.

## Why it's in the strategy
Works when the idea is better delivered through slides, visuals, diagrams, or screenshots than through text alone.

## When to use
- When the content is easier to take in one slide at a time than as a continuous block of text.
- When you have a piece that performed well on another platform and want to adapt it.

## How to build this type of post
1. Pick one topic that can be broken into discrete visual cards.
2. Make the first card immediately clear and compelling.
3. Give each card its own distinct point — no two cards should say the same thing.
4. Add caption text only where it adds meaning, not as a default.

## Possible applications
- Five mistakes made when building trust with a new client, one per card.
- A curated set of tools, books, or resources where each slide covers one item.

## What to keep in mind
- The first card does all the work of getting someone to swipe. Treat it as a standalone hook.
- Slides that are too text-heavy defeat the purpose of the format.
