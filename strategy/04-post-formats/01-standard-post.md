# Standard Post

## What is this
A single post up to ~500 characters.

## Why it's in the strategy
Best suited for one idea, a sharp observation, a strong claim, or a quick insight that doesn't need a long setup.

## When to use
- When you have one thesis and it needs no additional context.
- When the goal is speed of consumption and low friction to enter.

## How to build this type of post
1. Open with a strong first line — tension, contrast, or a concrete claim.
2. Hold to one idea and one conclusion.
3. Cut anything that doesn't change the meaning if removed.
4. The full post should be readable in a few seconds.

## Possible applications
- A short "key realization" about how momentum follows action, not the other way around.
- A contrarian claim with one supporting argument and one concrete takeaway.

## What to keep in mind
- Weak first lines kill this format. The hook carries everything.
- Don't try to pack two ideas in — it kills both.
