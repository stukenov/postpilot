# Thread

## What is this
A long-form post — a full argument, story, or breakdown that can't fit in a short format.

## Why it's in the strategy
The right format for a sequential argument, a case study, a personal story, or step-by-step logic that needs room to breathe.

## When to use
- When one idea requires progressive development across multiple beats.
- When you need to take the reader from setup to conclusion through several steps.

## How to build this type of post
1. Start with a hook strong enough to justify the longer format.
2. Follow a clear arc: problem → argument → conclusion.
3. Each section should pull the reader into the next.
4. Don't dilute the thread by mixing in competing ideas.

## Possible applications
- A deep breakdown of why consistent posting alone doesn't build an audience — and where the logic actually breaks.
- A personal story: the first failure, the turning point, the system that now works.

## What to keep in mind
- The hook has to work harder here than in any other format.
- One thread, one idea. Branching into a second point loses the reader.
