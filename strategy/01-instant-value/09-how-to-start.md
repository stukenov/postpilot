# How to Start

## What is this
Content for someone at zero — it removes the fear of beginning and shows the shortest path into a new topic or skill.

## Why it's in the strategy
This format expands your reach to beginners and captures people who have wanted to start for a while but don't know where the entry point is.

## When to use
- When a topic feels complex, overcrowded, or intimidating to outsiders.
- When the goal is to move someone from intention to a first concrete action.

## How to build this type of post
1. Name the reader's starting condition: no experience, no budget, no time, fear of failure.
2. Show the minimum viable set of steps to get started.
3. Defuse one or two of the most common fears or myths about beginning.
4. Tell them what not to do early on — to avoid burning out before they see results.

## Examples
- How to start freelancing with no portfolio and no existing clients.
- How to begin building in public when you're not an expert yet.
- Getting started with content creation when you have nothing to say (you think).
- How to start delegating when you've always done everything yourself.
- How to pick up a new skill as an adult without wasting months on the wrong resources.

## What to keep in mind
- Entry-level content should lower the barrier, not prove how hard the topic is.
- Concrete constraints work well: no experience, no audience, no budget, limited time.
