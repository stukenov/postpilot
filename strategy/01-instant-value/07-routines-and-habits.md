# Routines and Habits

## What is this
Content about repeatable behaviors that produce consistent results: daily habits, working rhythms, personal systems.

## Why it's in the strategy
Shows that outcomes come from a repeatable system, not talent or luck — and gives the reader something transferable they can adapt to their own context.

## When to use
- When you want to show the process behind a result, not just the result itself.
- When your audience needs to understand how to build change into their existing life.

## How to build this type of post
1. Pick one habit or set of habits tied to a specific, concrete outcome.
2. Explain how this routine actually produces that outcome — not just that it does.
3. Show how to start with a minimal version that doesn't require overhauling everything.
4. Give a signal: how will the reader know it's working?

## Examples
- The weekly review routine that keeps my projects from slipping through the cracks.
- Three habits that separate consultants who grow their practice from those who plateau.
- How I write every day without making it a struggle.
- The one habit that quietly increases client retention without extra outreach.
- What I cut from my mornings to get back two productive hours.

## What to keep in mind
- Don't romanticize the routine — the reader needs a system, not an aesthetic.
- Describe habits through action and effect, not abstract values or identity claims.
