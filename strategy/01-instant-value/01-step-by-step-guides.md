# Step-by-Step Guides

## What is this
A format where you promise a clear outcome and walk the reader through a specific sequence of actions to reach it.

## Why it's in the strategy
This type of post demonstrates practical value immediately and works well for attracting people who need a measurable result, not more theory.

## When to use
- When your audience has an urgent task and needs a clear path forward.
- When you want to show expertise through structure rather than self-promotion.

## How to build this type of post
1. State the promised result in the opening line.
2. Break the path into 3–7 steps with concrete actions.
3. Remove background theory — keep only what the reader can do immediately.
4. Close with a short takeaway or the single easiest first step.

## Examples
- How to write a business proposal in one afternoon without starting from scratch.
- How to prepare a month of content in under an hour.
- How to land your first consulting client with no existing audience.
- How to run a meaningful retrospective when your team is burned out.
- How to cut your weekly meeting load in half without losing alignment.

## What to keep in mind
- The more specific the timeframe, number, or outcome, the stronger the hook.
- Don't turn a guide into a survey of the whole topic — one problem, one path.
