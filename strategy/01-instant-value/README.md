# Step 1. Instant Value

This section handles first contact. The reader doesn't know you yet and only cares about one thing: what's in it for them right now — a fast solution, a clear outcome, practical help.

## Section Focus
- Goal: Capture attention and deliver immediate value.
- Role in the funnel: Entry point for cold audiences coming in from referrals or discovery.

## How to Use This Folder
- Use these formats as your content foundation when your audience is small and you need to prove usefulness first.
- Pick topics from your niche to attract potential clients, not random viewers.
- Every post in this step should answer: "What do I get right now?"

## Formats Inside

- [Step-by-step guides](01-step-by-step-guides.md) — Walk the reader through a clear sequence of actions toward a specific result.
- [Checklists](02-checklists.md) — Compressed lists of actions or criteria the reader can save and reuse.
- [Curated lists](03-curated-lists.md) — Tools, resources, or ideas grouped around one clear task.
- [Problem breakdowns](04-problem-breakdowns.md) — Take one recognizable painful situation and show how to handle it.
- [Controversial takes](05-controversial-takes.md) — A sharp claim that challenges the common view and demands engagement.
- [Best practices](06-best-practices.md) — One strong technique or exercise that delivers a quick, concrete win.
- [Routines and habits](07-routines-and-habits.md) — Repeatable behaviors that produce consistent results.
- [Lessons from experience](08-lessons-from-experience.md) — Distilled takeaways from personal tests, experiments, or client work.
- [How to start](09-how-to-start.md) — Lowest-barrier entry point for beginners who want to act but don't know where to begin.

## Important Notes
- The strategy starts with value, not with personal stories or entertainment.
- For a small audience, this step should make up the majority of your content.
