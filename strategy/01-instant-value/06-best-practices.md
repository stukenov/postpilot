# Best Practices

## What is this
A post where you share one strong tool, technique, or exercise that produces a quick, concrete effect.

## Why it's in the strategy
It gives the reader a small win immediately and connects that win to your approach — building trust through direct experience, not claims.

## When to use
- When you have one technique the reader can apply right now, without setup.
- When the audience needs a low-barrier entry point before committing to a bigger change.

## How to build this type of post
1. Describe the situation where this technique is most useful.
2. Give a clear step-by-step application.
3. State what result to expect and over what timeframe.
4. If relevant, note the limits: who it won't work for and what it doesn't replace.

## Examples
- The five-minute end-of-day review that stops work anxiety from bleeding into evenings.
- One question to ask before any meeting that cuts the agenda in half.
- A simple reframe that makes difficult client feedback easier to act on.
- The writing habit that produces a first draft in a single session.
- How to use a constraint to break creative blocks when you're stuck on a project.

## What to keep in mind
- The technique must be genuinely executable without buying anything or hiring anyone.
- The strongest structure: when to use it → how to do it → what you'll notice.
