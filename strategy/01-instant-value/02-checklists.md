# Checklists

## What is this
A compressed set of actions, criteria, or reminders the reader can save and use as a reference.

## Why it's in the strategy
Checklists are easy to consume and easy to share, which makes them effective for reach and for building a reputation as a useful, organized expert.

## When to use
- When a topic can be broken into short points without needing long explanations.
- When the value of the post is helping the reader not miss anything and verify quickly.

## How to build this type of post
1. Gather 3–10 items around one specific task.
2. Write each point in plain, direct language — how the reader thinks, not how a textbook reads.
3. Add one clarifying line per item if needed.
4. End in a way that makes the reader want to save the post.

## Examples
- 7 things to check before sending a client proposal.
- 5 questions to ask in any job interview (that most candidates skip).
- The onboarding checklist every new hire should receive on day one.
- 8 signals your landing page is costing you conversions.
- Before you launch: 10 things most founders forget.

## What to keep in mind
- One focused list beats a broad collection of loosely related tips.
- Each item should be useful on its own, not only as part of the full list.
