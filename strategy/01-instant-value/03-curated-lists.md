# Curated Lists

## What is this
Posts that collect tools, resources, ideas, frameworks, or ready-made solutions around one clear task.

## Why it's in the strategy
They remove the paralysis of too many options and demonstrate that you can orient someone quickly — a key signal of expertise.

## When to use
- When your audience doesn't know what to pick or where to start looking.
- When you want to deliver high value without a long explanation.

## How to build this type of post
1. Name the specific task the list solves.
2. Select only what's relevant — don't include everything, include the right things.
3. Add a short note on who each item works best for or when to use it.
4. Where possible, include your selection criteria or a personal opinion.

## Examples
- 5 tools I use to run a solo consulting practice without an assistant.
- The only 3 books on pricing that actually changed how I work with clients.
- Frameworks for structuring a discovery call (and when to use each one).
- 6 newsletters worth reading if you work in product.
- Free resources that replaced $500/month in software for my team.

## What to keep in mind
- A curated list is strongest when your selection criteria and perspective are visible.
- Don't let it become a faceless link dump — your editorial judgment is the value.
