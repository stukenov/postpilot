# Problem Breakdowns

## What is this
Content where you take one recognizable, painful situation and break down how to handle it.

## Why it's in the strategy
This format lets the reader see themselves in the scenario and positions you as someone who understands context — not just theory.

## When to use
- When your audience keeps running into the same conflicts, fears, or dead ends.
- When abstract advice lands worse than a single concrete case worked through.

## How to build this type of post
1. Describe a specific situation the reader can recognize themselves in.
2. Show what most people do wrong and why it doesn't work.
3. Offer a more effective response, sequence of actions, or reframe.
4. End with one clear takeaway the reader can apply immediately.

## Examples
- How to handle a client who keeps expanding scope without paying more.
- What to do when a team member ignores deadlines repeatedly.
- How to respond when a prospect says "we'll think about it" and goes silent.
- What to do when your best employee puts in their notice.
- How to recover a project that's behind schedule without losing the client's trust.

## What to keep in mind
- The format is strongest when you break down a vivid, specific scene — not a broad category of problems.
- Avoid moralizing: the reader needs a foothold, not a lecture.
