# Best Posting Times

## What is this
There is no universal best time to post. The idea that a specific hour unlocks reach is a myth.

## Why it's in the strategy
This principle protects against false optimization — spending more energy on the calendar than on the content itself.

## When to use
- When you're tempted to blame weak results on timing rather than content quality.
- When scheduling is taking more effort than writing.

## How to apply
1. Post during reasonable hours for your own workflow — daytime is fine.
2. Do not build your strategy around specific time slots.
3. Evaluate content first: quality, relevance, and strength of the angle.
4. Treat timing as a secondary factor, not a lever to rescue weak posts.

## Examples
- Audiences on social platforms span multiple time zones — a strong post can gain traction at any hour.
- A mediocre post published at the "optimal" time still underperforms a sharp post published at noon.

## What to keep in mind
- Low reach is almost always a content problem, not a timing problem.
