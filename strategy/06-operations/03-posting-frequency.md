# Posting Frequency

## What is this
There is no ideal number. The more strong content you produce, the higher the chance of growth, virality, and sales — as long as quality holds.

## Why it's in the strategy
This reframes the question from "what's the right frequency?" to "what volume can I sustain without dropping quality?"

## When to use
- When you're searching for a perfect posting schedule instead of building a repeatable system.
- When volume is starting to hurt quality, or when you're posting too infrequently to see growth.

## How to apply
1. Post as much as you can sustain over weeks without quality dropping.
2. Track what volume you can actually hold consistently — not just for one good week.
3. If you have capacity, increase the number of strong posts, not just the total post count.
4. Don't expect one post per week to produce reliable growth.

## Examples
- Publishing 1–2 posts a day for months with no growth is a common pattern. Increasing to 5–10 strong posts a day has produced measurable audience growth within days for some creators.
- The constraint is always long-term sustainability, not short-term burst output.

## What to keep in mind
- More posts only help if quality stays consistent. Volume without quality accelerates irrelevance.
