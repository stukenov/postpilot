# Operations

Practical answers to the questions that come up once you have a strategy but need to execute it consistently: when to post, how much of each content type, where ideas come from, and how to sell without ruining reach.

## Section focus
- Goal: Collect working rules that affect publishing cadence, idea flow, and conversion.
- Role in the funnel: Cross-cutting operational decisions that sit on top of the full strategy.

## How to use this folder
- Come back here when you're stuck on process, not ideas.
- These rules don't replace strategy steps — they help you execute them reliably.
- When in doubt between speed and quality, always choose quality and sustainability.
