# How to Sell

## What is this
A recommended funnel structure: use your public feed to attract the right audience, then convert through a dedicated channel — newsletter, community, DMs, or landing page — rather than inside every post.

## Why it's in the strategy
This approach keeps reach intact by not loading every post with a direct pitch, while still driving consistent movement toward a sale.

## When to use
- When you need to embed selling without burning reach or turning your feed into an ad channel.
- When you have a secondary platform or touchpoint for deeper engagement and conversion.

## How to apply
1. Lead with strong, targeted content in your public feed so followers want to learn more.
2. Direct interested readers to your newsletter, community, or landing page — via your profile bio or a comment, not mid-post.
3. On that secondary platform, make the product and its value immediately visible.
4. Don't add a call to action to every post, and don't drop links into posts that are still gaining early traction.
5. Once the basic funnel works, add a lead magnet — a free resource that warms up the next step.

## Examples
- A post that attracts significant traffic can drive substantial newsletter signups and direct inquiries in a single day, when the funnel is clear.
- Placing a link in a comment rather than in the post body preserves algorithmic reach on most platforms.

## What to keep in mind
- Sending people from an off-topic post to a sales page doesn't work. The content that drives someone to your funnel must match what you're selling.
