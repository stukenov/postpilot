# Content Mix

## What is this
The smaller the audience, the more value-driven the content should be. Entertainment and personality content can be layered in later, once expertise is established.

## Why it's in the strategy
This rule sets clear priorities: prove your value first, then expand your range and tone.

## When to use
- When your audience is still growing and hasn't yet understood why they should follow you.
- When you're tempted to shift into lifestyle or personality content before expertise is anchored.

## How to apply
1. On a small or early-stage account, prioritize useful, targeted content.
2. Add personality or entertainment posts carefully — don't let them blur your positioning.
3. Keep 70–80% of content inside the real needs of your target audience at any stage.
4. Judge each post not by genre but by whether it moves someone closer to trusting and hiring you.

## Examples
- Large accounts with established authority can afford more varied, off-topic content because credibility carries them.
- An early-stage account needs to first answer: "Why should I follow this person, and how will this help me?"

## What to keep in mind
- Avoid attracting a random audience. Every post should pull in someone who might eventually become a client.
