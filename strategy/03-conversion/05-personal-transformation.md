# Personal Transformation

## What is this
The story of your own journey — a shift, a turning point, or a before-and-after — connected to your current expertise.

## Why it's in the strategy
Adds human context, strengthens trust, and shows that you're not a detached theorist but someone who has actually lived the path.

## When to use
- When your personal history explains where your expertise and motivation come from.
- When the audience needs to see that change is possible for someone like them.

## How to build this type of post
1. Describe your starting point honestly — skip the heroism.
2. Identify the turning point or the chain of steps that changed things.
3. Show what shifted — in your thinking, your approach, or your results.
4. Connect the story to what this means for the clients you work with now.

## Examples
- My first client paid $50 for something I now charge $5,000 for — here's what changed.
- I built the first version in a weekend and was embarrassed by it. That version got the first 100 users.
- I spent two years giving advice I hadn't tested myself. Then I ran the experiment. Everything I thought I knew was wrong.
- I used to over-deliver to avoid conflict. Learning to say no was the skill that made the business work.

## What to keep in mind
- Transformation stories earn trust through vulnerability and specificity, not through self-glorification.
- The strongest versions connect your past struggle directly to what you now help clients with.
