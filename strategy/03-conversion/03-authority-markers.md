# Authority Markers

## What is this
Posts featuring external trust signals: awards, notable clients, speaking roles, rankings, published work, or professional recognition.

## Why it's in the strategy
Confirms that your expertise has been validated by others — the market, peers, or institutions — not just claimed by you.

## When to use
- When you have external credentials that genuinely raise trust in your niche.
- When the audience needs a social signal about your level before committing.

## How to build this type of post
1. Pick one strong authority marker.
2. Briefly explain why it matters in your specific context.
3. Don't stack all your credentials at once — one per post.
4. Connect the marker to what it means for the client, not just for your resume.

## Examples
- Invited to speak at an industry conference with 500+ attendees.
- Listed in a top-10 ranking for consultants in this space.
- My clients include brands you've heard of — here's what I learned working at that scale.
- My framework was featured in a major industry publication last year.

## What to keep in mind
- Authority markers should help the reader make a decision, not just impress them.
- One strong, relevant marker beats ten weak or generic ones.
