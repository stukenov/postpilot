# Unique Approach

## What is this
Posts about how you work — your process, your rules, your style — and what sets you apart from others who do similar things.

## Why it's in the strategy
Helps the reader decide whether your way of working fits them, beyond just the outcome they're after.

## When to use
- When the market is crowded and you need to show your specific method, not just your results.
- When people have doubts about what working with you actually feels like day-to-day.

## How to build this type of post
1. Pick one distinctive feature of your approach.
2. Explain why it exists and what benefit it creates for the client.
3. Show who this approach is especially well-suited for.
4. If relevant, name who it is NOT for — this builds more trust, not less.

## Examples
- My clients ask any question without judgment — there are no "stupid" questions in our work together.
- I keep sessions under 45 minutes because attention is the resource, not time.
- I don't build slide decks with 80 rules. One sharp insight per session, applied immediately.
- My system is built so the whole thing runs in under 30 minutes a day.

## What to keep in mind
- A distinctive approach lands harder when it's tied to client benefit, not just self-expression.
- Explicitly excluding the wrong-fit client increases trust with the right one.
