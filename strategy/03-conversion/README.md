# Step 3. Conversion

After building trust and shifting perspective, the audience needs to see what makes you specifically worth choosing — your proof, your values, and your results. No one will assume your strength if you never show it.

## Section focus
- Goal: Show why someone should choose you over similar options.
- Funnel role: Move attention from "this is interesting" to "I want to work with this person."

## How to use this section
- Apply this step once baseline trust exists and the audience understands the problem you solve.
- Show facts, proof, and case studies — not just your own opinion of yourself.
- Conversion content should help people make a decision, not just ask them to buy.
