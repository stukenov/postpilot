# Social Proof

## What is this
Evidence of results through numbers, case studies, client outcomes, or team wins.

## Why it's in the strategy
Removes the doubt "will this even work for me?" and shifts the conversation from promises to demonstrated outcomes.

## When to use
- When you have measurable results from clients, projects, or your own work.
- When the audience needs to see proof, not theory.

## How to build this type of post
1. Pick one type of proof: a case study, a testimonial, a number, a before/after.
2. State the result as concretely as possible.
3. Add context where you can: timeframe, starting point, conditions.
4. Draw a conclusion about who this is relevant for and why it worked.

## Examples
- 70% of participants landed a new role within 60 days of finishing the program.
- Managed $500K in ad spend over 6 months with a team of 3.
- Grew an audience from zero to 15,000 in 5 months without paid promotion.
- 200+ clients have gone through this process — here's what the first 30 days typically look like.

## What to keep in mind
- Less framing around a case study makes the result hit harder.
- Numbers without context are weaker than numbers with a clear starting point.
